The Octopus

Octopuses are soft-bodied cephalopods belonging to the order Octopoda. They are among the most intelligent invertebrates and have complex nervous systems.

They have three hearts and blue blood due to the copper-based protein hemocyanin, which is more efficient than hemoglobin for oxygen transport in cold, low-oxygen environments.

Octopuses are known for their remarkable ability to change color and texture, allowing them to camouflage with their surroundings in milliseconds.

Most species are solitary and live in dens made from rocks or other materials on the ocean floor. They are carnivorous, feeding on crabs, clams, and small fish.
